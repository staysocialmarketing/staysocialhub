Your bank is going to send you a renewal letter. If your mortgage matures in the next 12 months, it may already be on its way. Most people sign it.

Before you do, here are three questions worth asking.

Is this rate the best available to me, or just the best your bank is offering? These are different questions. Banks send renewal rates based on their posted terms. A broker compares those terms against dozens of lenders and finds what actually fits your situation. That conversation is worth having before you sign anything.

Does the term I am signing make sense for where rates might go? A five-year fixed locks you in at today's rate. A shorter term or a variable might work better depending on your situation and your plans over the next few years. That decision is worth thinking through, not defaulting to.

What happens if I need to break this mortgage before it matures? The penalty answer is buried in the fine print and it is usually larger than people expect. Knowing it ahead of time matters.
