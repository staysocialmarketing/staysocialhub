The Bank of Canada cut the overnight rate by 25 basis points today, bringing it to 2.50%. I want to give you the honest picture, not just the headline.

If you're in an alternative lending product right now, your rate didn't drop by 25bps today. Alternative lenders don't price directly off the overnight rate the same way the big banks do. The spread still exists between where you're borrowing today and where you'll land when you qualify conventionally. That's the reality of alternative lending, and it's worth being clear about.

What this cut does change: the conventional mortgage market you're working toward is getting more accessible. When you're ready to move, you're moving into a lower-rate environment than you were twelve months ago. On a Toronto mortgage, that's a meaningful difference in your monthly payment and in your qualifying calculation.

For privately mortgaged clients approaching their term: this rate cut changes the landscape for what's available at renewal. We should talk before your term ends, not after.

For clients currently rebuilding their credit file: stay the course. Your next step is about consistency and time, not about rate announcements. I'm tracking your file, and when the conditions are right, we'll make the move.

The path to A qualification isn't fast, but today's cut made the other end of it a little better.
